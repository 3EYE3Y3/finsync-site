Send feedback

Thanks for testing FinSync. Bug reports from different players and devices are the most useful thing you can send.

Report a problem or idea

Open an issue on GitHub

Needs a free GitHub account. If you installed FinSync from Google Play, you can also leave private feedback from FinSync's page in the Play Store.

What helps most

  • Your device (e.g. HiBy R4, FiiO M11, a Pixel phone) and Android version
  • The music player you use (Poweramp, HiBy Music, USB Audio Player PRO…)
  • FinSync's version (Settings → Apps → FinSync) and your Jellyfin server version
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Please don't include your server address, user name or passwords.
